## What it means

1. **Realism (honest limitation).** Unit prices are drawn uniformly from a per-currency range (`price()` in `auditenv/generator/story.py`), so their first digits follow the ranges, not Benford (MAD 0.0220, nonconformity). Quantities, sums of several lines and currency conversion mix the digits: invoice totals MAD 0.0131 (marginal), the ledger as a whole 0.0031 (close). Real receivables often do not follow Benford either when prices sit in a narrow band, so this is a realism note, not a bug. A log-uniform price draw would make the books Benford-like; it changes every episode byte, so it is listed in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
2. **No shortcut.** Rows of affected documents and clean rows have nearly the same digit pattern: MAD between them 0.0025. Errors are injected by copying or re-posting real amounts, so a Benford test cannot point at them - the agent has to reconcile.
